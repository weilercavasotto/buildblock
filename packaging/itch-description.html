BuildBlock is a 3D building game. Snap bricks together stud by stud, mount wheels, engines, wings and rotors on them, then press Play and drive, fly or sail your creation under real physics.

How it works

  1. Build: pick parts from the catalog and place them on the grid. Blocks, plates, slopes, tiles, round parts and glass connect through their studs.
  2. Wire it up: you decide which key runs what. Give your engines, wheels, thrusters and pistons their own keys.
  3. Play: your creation becomes a physical machine. Drive it, crash it, press Stop and tweak it until it works.

Features

  • Lots of parts: blocks, plates, slopes and L brackets in many sizes, tiles, round bricks, glass, four wheel sizes and skis.
  • Mobility parts: engines, turbos, thrusters, propellers, rotors, pistons, springs, hinges and swept wings for planes and helicopters.
  • Lights and sirens: headlamps and flashing sirens, with matching sounds.
  • Tune everything: engine power, tire grip, suspension, brake strength, block weight and spring stiffness are sliders away.
  • Paint your build: a full palette and a paint mode to colour every part.
  • Comfortable editing: box selection, move and rotate, undo / redo, and four cursor modes: inspect, build, paint and remove.
  • Saved games: as many creations as you like, each with a picture and its own floor size.
  • Built-in tutorial and help: a short guided tour for your first game and a help window one key away (H).
  • Everything synthesised: all sounds are generated live, so the game stays small and loads fast.

Controls

  • Left click: place (drag to keep placing)
  • Right click: remove, right drag: pan
  • Middle drag: orbit, scroll: zoom
  • A / D and W / S: rotate and tilt the part you are placing
  • I / B / E / R: inspect / build / paint / remove
  • C: parts catalog, 1-9: hotbar, Ctrl/Cmd+Z / Y: undo / redo
  • M: mute, H: help

In Play mode no part has a default key: assign the keys you like to your engines, wheels and other parts, and the controls panel shows what each key does.
